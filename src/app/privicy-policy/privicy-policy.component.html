Enforce Solutions App is used to manage the attendance of employees within an organization. An employee of an organization can mark his attendance in various ways depending on the employee's role. In-Office Checkin is for Knowledge workers and skilled professionals. Place Checkin for Supervisors and Drivers who move from one Location to Location and Sale Persons. ( Enforce app collects location data in the background to verify distance traveled by employees even when the app is closed or not in use.)


Location Permission

Enforce Solutions App Background location capture only takes place when the user of the app performs checkout from a location where the user is prompted with two options. The first option is Checkout If a user selects checkout then the background location is captured until the user has marked a check-in in another location and the user can view travel data on a map. The second option is "end of the day " when the user has confirmed it's the end of the workday.
